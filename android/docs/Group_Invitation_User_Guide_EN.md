# GROUP INVITATION USER GUIDE
## Creating, Sharing, and Joining Groups in Disaster App

### 1. Introduction

**Group Invitation** is a feature in Disaster App that allows users to invite others to join a Group through a Nearby Room.

Instead of copying and sharing a Group invitation link with each person individually, you can create an invitation with a title and description and share it directly in a Nearby Room conversation.

Anyone who can view the public invitation can learn about the Group and tap **Join** to start the joining process.

### 2. Understanding Nearby Rooms and Groups

**Nearby Room** is a conversation space where users connected through the app's Nearby functionality can communicate and exchange information. It also provides a place to share Group invitations.

**Group** is a separate communication group with its own identity and access credentials. Joining a Nearby Room does not automatically make you a member of any Group shared within that room.

For example, a Nearby Room may contain invitations to several Groups, such as emergency response teams, volunteer coordination groups, or local community communication groups.

### 3. How to Create and Share a Group Invitation

**Step 1 — Open a Nearby Room**

Open a Nearby Room you are participating in and access the Group Invitation creation feature.

**Step 2 — Select a Group**

Choose an existing Group on your device that you want to invite others to join.

The app uses the Group's existing access information to generate the invitation.

**Step 3 — Enter the Invitation Details**

Provide the following information:

- **Group:** The Group you want to share.
- **Title:** A short, descriptive invitation title.
- **Description:** Information about the Group's purpose, activities, and intended participants.

Example:

**Title:** Local Emergency Response Group

**Description:** A community group for sharing emergency information, coordinating assistance, and staying connected during emergencies in the local area.

**Step 4 — Preview and Send**

Review the invitation details, then confirm to send it to the Nearby Room.

The invitation will appear in the conversation, where other room participants can view it.

**Important:** Sending an invitation does not automatically add recipients to the Group. Each person must choose to join.

### 4. How to View Group Invitations

When someone shares a Group Invitation, it appears in the Nearby Room conversation with information such as:

- The sender's avatar and display name.
- The time the invitation was sent.
- The invitation title and description.
- A **Join** button.

You can tap the invitation content to open **Invitations in Room**.

This screen displays the currently visible invitations in chronological order, from oldest to newest. When opened from a specific invitation, the list automatically scrolls to that invitation.

Tap an invitation in the list to open its detailed view.

### 5. How to Join a Group from an Invitation

**Step 1 — Find an Invitation**

You can access an invitation directly from the Nearby Room conversation, the Invitations in Room list, or the invitation detail screen.

**Step 2 — Tap Join**

Tap **Join** on the invitation for the Group you want to join.

Disaster App retrieves the corresponding invitation link and opens the existing Group Join flow.

You do not need to manually copy or enter the link.

**Step 3 — Confirm Your Membership**

Review the information displayed on the Group Join screen and follow the app's instructions to confirm joining.

**Step 4 — Open the Group**

Once the joining process succeeds, you can access the Group and begin communicating with its members.

### 6. Hiding and Restoring Invitations

If you no longer want to see an invitation in your conversation, you can use **Hide**.

After hiding an invitation:

- Its full content is no longer displayed in the conversation.
- Its original message position is preserved.
- A placeholder indicates that the invitation has been hidden and provides an **Unhide** option.
- The hidden invitation is excluded from the list of currently visible invitations.

To restore the invitation, tap **Unhide** in the hidden message placeholder.

**Hide and Unhide only affect the invitation's visibility on your device.** They do not delete the original invitation, revoke access credentials, or change what other participants can see.

### 7. Security and Privacy Considerations

A Group Invitation may contain access credentials required to join a Group. Keep the following points in mind:

- Share invitations only in Nearby Rooms whose participants are appropriate for the intended Group.
- Do not treat a Public Invitation as a private invitation addressed to a specific person.
- Anyone who obtains valid invitation credentials may be able to share them with others.
- Join only Groups whose purpose and invitation source you trust.
- Do not assume hiding an invitation revokes access to the Group.

**Public Invitations are not an approval-based membership system.** Users with valid invitation credentials can use the existing Group Join mechanism, subject to the app's applicable access policies.

### 8. Frequently Asked Questions

**Does sending an invitation automatically add Nearby Room participants to the Group?**

No. Each recipient must tap Join and complete the joining process.

**Can I join a Group without manually entering an invitation link?**

Yes. Tapping Join on a Public Invitation automatically opens the existing Group Join flow with the corresponding invitation link.

**Can I review invitations previously shared in a Nearby Room?**

Yes. Invitations that remain visible can be viewed in the conversation and the Invitations in Room list.

**Does hiding an invitation remove it for everyone?**

No. Hiding an invitation only changes its visibility on your device.

**Can I join multiple Groups shared in the same Nearby Room?**

Yes, provided each invitation remains valid and the corresponding joining process satisfies the app's existing access requirements.

### 9. Summary

Group Invitation makes community coordination in Disaster App easier by connecting two existing capabilities:

**Nearby Rooms help people discover and communicate with one another. Group Invitations help them move from a shared conversation into a dedicated communication Group.**

The process is straightforward:

**For senders:** Select a Group → Create an Invitation → Share it in a Nearby Room.

**For recipients:** View the Invitation → Tap Join → Confirm → Open the Group.

This feature reuses Disaster App's existing invitation-link-based Group Join mechanism, allowing users to join Groups without manually handling invitation links.

---

*Scope: This guide describes Public Group Invitations shared in Nearby Rooms. Private Invitations use different delivery and access rules and are not covered in this guide.*
