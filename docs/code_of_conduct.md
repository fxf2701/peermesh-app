# PeerMesh Code of Conduct

PeerMesh welcomes respectful, constructive collaboration. This document sets expectations for everyone participating in the project.

## Scope

These guidelines apply to issues, pull requests, discussions, and project-related communication channels. They do not cover private interactions unrelated to the project.

## Expected Behavior

- Communicate politely and give specific, useful feedback.
- Discuss technical ideas and implementation choices without personal attacks.
- Respect different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project and describe software capabilities and limitations accurately.
- Respect other contributors' time, availability, and decisions about which tasks to take on.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Repeated unwanted contact or pressure to participate in tasks or activities.
- Sharing personal or identifying information without permission.
- Spam or unrelated promotional content.

## Reporting a Concern

Contact the project maintainers through an available project contact channel. Include the relevant context and links where possible.

Do not include personal or confidential information in a public report. If a concern involves sensitive details, ask a maintainer for a private way to share them. Maintainers should handle reports discreetly and respectfully.

## Moderation

Maintainers may ask participants to change their behavior, remove inappropriate content, close discussions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when these guidelines are violated.

Responses should reflect the nature and severity of the behavior. Material updates to these guidelines will be recorded in the repository history.
