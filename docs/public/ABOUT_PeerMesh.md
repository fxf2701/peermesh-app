## About Peer Mesh

Peer Mesh is an offline-first chat app that allows nearby devices to communicate without internet access.

It uses local device-to-device networking technologies such as Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct to create a temporary mesh network.

No account. No server. Just nearby communication.

---

## When to Use Peer Mesh

Peer Mesh can be useful in situations such as:

- During travel in areas with weak signal  
- At festivals, events, or crowded places  
- On-site teamwork without stable connectivity  
- Temporary internet outages  
- Emergency situations where networks are unavailable  

Peer Mesh is designed for everyday local communication.

---

## How It Works

Peer Mesh connects nearby devices directly using local wireless technologies.

Messages can be relayed across multiple devices to extend communication range within a local area.

Communication remains entirely on local devices unless you choose otherwise.

---

## Privacy

Peer Mesh does not require account registration.

Messages are stored locally on your device.

The app does not collect personal information.
